# Working Principle

The self-watering plant system operates using a passive water-transfer concept.

1. Water is stored in a separate reservoir.
2. The water-transfer mechanism connects the reservoir to the plant-growing medium.
3. Water gradually moves towards the soil.
4. The soil receives water through the connected mechanism.
5. The water level and soil moisture are monitored during testing.

The system aims to reduce the need for frequent manual watering.

**Note:** The exact water-transfer mechanism depends on the materials and design used in the prototype.
