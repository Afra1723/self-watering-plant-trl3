# Future Development Plan

## Phase 1 – Design Refinement

- Improve the reservoir structure.
- Refine the water-transfer mechanism.
- Improve material selection.

## Phase 2 – Functional Improvements

- Explore moisture sensor integration.
- Consider automatic water-level monitoring.
- Improve water distribution.

## Phase 3 – Performance Evaluation

- Develop a structured validation procedure.
- Evaluate water consumption.
- Assess soil moisture consistency.
- Examine system reliability.

## Phase 4 – Advanced Development

- Explore IoT-based monitoring.
- Consider automated irrigation control.
- Investigate scalable and affordable manufacturing possibilities.

## Final Goal

Develop an affordable, reliable and sustainable self-watering system.
