# Project Design

## 1. Design Description

The self-watering plant system is designed as a simple, low-cost irrigation prototype.

## 2. Main Components

- Water storage reservoir
- Water-transfer mechanism
- Plant pot
- Soil or growing medium
- Plant

*Update this list to match the actual materials used in your prototype.*

## 3. Design Requirements

1. The reservoir should hold sufficient water for testing.
2. Water should transfer gradually towards the soil.
3. The system should remain stable during operation.
4. Water leakage should be monitored.
5. The design should be affordable and easy to maintain.

## 4. Expected Function

The integrated components should work together to provide a gradual water supply to the plant.

## 5. Design Improvements

Future improvements may include better reservoir capacity, improved water distribution and moisture monitoring.
