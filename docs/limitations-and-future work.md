# Limitations and Future Work

## 1. Current Limitations

The self-watering plant system has certain design limitations:

- Water supply depends on the water-transfer mechanism.
- Reservoir capacity determines water storage.
- Water distribution may vary depending on soil conditions.
- The system does not currently include automatic moisture monitoring.
- Long-term performance has not been experimentally evaluated.

## 2. Possible Improvements

1. Improve the water reservoir design.
2. Develop a more consistent water-transfer mechanism.
3. Introduce a soil moisture sensor.
4. Explore automatic water-level monitoring.
5. Improve the overall stability and durability of the prototype.

## 3. Future Scope

The project can be further developed into a smart irrigation system by integrating sensors and automated water control.

## 4. Conclusion

The project explores an affordable and simple approach to plant watering. Further development could improve its functionality, efficiency and usability.
