# Self-Watering Plant System

### Low-Cost Sustainable Irrigation Prototype

**Project Category:** Sustainable Innovation / Agriculture Technology  
**Technology Readiness Level:** TRL 3 – Initial Proof of Concept  
**Development Target:** TRL 4 – Laboratory Validation

## 1. Project Overview

The Self-Watering Plant System is a low-cost plant irrigation concept designed to supply water to a plant gradually using a simple water reservoir and a passive water-transfer mechanism.

The project aims to reduce frequent manual watering and explore a simple, affordable approach to plant care.

## 2. Problem Statement

Plants require regular watering to maintain suitable growing conditions. Manual watering can be inconvenient, particularly when users are occupied or unavailable.

This project explores an affordable solution that can provide a more consistent water supply.

## 3. Objectives

- Develop a low-cost self-watering prototype.
- Explore passive water transfer to the soil.
- Reduce frequent manual watering.
- Observe water consumption and soil moisture.
- Evaluate prototype functionality through controlled testing.

## 4. Proposed Working Principle

The prototype uses a water reservoir connected to the plant-growing medium through a water-transfer mechanism.

Water moves gradually from the reservoir towards the soil, helping maintain moisture around the plant.

## 5. Prototype Development

The initial prototype was developed using accessible materials and a simple design.

Photographs and detailed construction information will be added to the documentation.

## 6. Experimental Validation

The prototype will undergo controlled indoor testing.

Parameters to be observed:

- Initial and final reservoir water levels.
- Changes in soil moisture.
- Duration of water supply.
- Consistency of water transfer.
- Overall prototype functionality.

## 7. Technology Readiness

Current stage: TRL 3 – Proof of Concept.

Target stage: TRL 4 – Component and breadboard validation in a laboratory environment.

TRL 4 will only be claimed after successful integration and documented experimental validation.

## 8. Future Improvements

- Improve reservoir design.
- Improve water-transfer consistency.
- Evaluate water efficiency.
- Conduct repeated experiments.
- Explore moisture sensor integration.

## 9. Project Status

Prototype development completed. Experimental validation and documentation are in progress.
